Eidolon is a soul-bound RPG where an AI companion — your Eidolon — adventures alongside you and evolves with your real life.

KEY FEATURES

• AI-Generated Dungeons — Every dungeon is uniquely crafted by AI. No two runs are the same.

• A Living Companion — Your Eidolon has a personality shaped by your onboarding quiz. It remembers your journey and responds with AI-driven dialogue.

• Reality Sync — Connect Health Connect (optional) to convert your real steps, workouts, and sleep into in-game ATK, HP, and EXP bonuses. Your real-world effort powers your Eidolon.

• Soul Summon — Summon fragments of your soul through a transparent gacha system. All rates are disclosed before every pull.

• Built for Privacy — Health data is used only to compute bonuses and is never sold. Delete your account and data anytime from Settings.
